# How to Approach HR:

## LinkedIn Approach
   1. Find the HR/Recruiter
      - Search: Company Name + HR/Recruiter on LinkedIn
   2. Send a Connection Note
      - "Hi, I am a final year B.Tech CS Student. I would love to connect and
         explore opportunities at your company."
   3. Follow-up Message:
      - "Thank you for connecting. Please find my resume attached. I would love to
         discuss any suitable openings."
   4. Follow Up Once Only
      - If no reply in 5-7 days, send ONE polite follow-up.
      - Never more than twice.

## Email Approach:
   1. Good Email Template [Keep it short]

      Subject: Application for Software Engineer
   
      Dear [HR Name]
   
      I am a final year B.Tech CS student with skills in Python and full-stack development.
      I am writing to express my interest in software engineer roles at your organization.
   
      I have attached my resume for reference. I would be grateful for an opportunity to
      discuss how I can contribute.
   
      Warm regards, [Your Name]

   
   2. Never do this
      - Say 'Please give me a job urgently'
      - Use 'Hii' or informal greetings
      - Copy Paste to 50 HRs at once
      - Message at odd hours like midnight


# Types of Interviews: Know exactly what to expect in each round

  1. Round 1 - Screening 
     - Telephonic Interview
     - 10-15 min screening by HR. Tests basic fit.
     - Questions about yourself & availability
     - Salary expectations & location preference
     - Keep resume in front of you
     - Find a quiet place. Speak slowly and clearly.

  2. Round 2 - HR Round
     - Tests personality, attitube & culture fit
     - Tell me about yourself
     - Strengths, weakness & motivation
     - Why this company?
     - Be confident and enthusiastic. This round is about WHO you are.

  3. Round 3 - Skills
     - Tests subject knowledge & problem solving
     - DSA coding & domain concepts
     - Explain your projects in detail
     - Live problem solving
     - Be honest. Say 'I don't know but i'll learn.' Never bluff

  4. Round 4 - Final
     - Multiple Interviews - HR + Tech + Manager
     - Mix of HR & technical questions
     - Pressure testing & group assessment
     - Make eye contact with ALL panelists
     - Stay clam. Take a breadth before answering. Address everyone


# How to Introduct Yourself: The 5-Part Formula - 60-90 sec, practiced and confident
  1. Who you are?
     - "I am Mohammad Aslam, a final year B.Tech Computer Science student from MAKAUT".

  2. Your Key Skills
     - "I specialize in Python, full-stack development using React & Node.js, and SQL
        database management."

  3. Experience & Projects
     - "I built an e-commerce platform using Django handling 500+ users.
        I interned at XYZ Tech building REST APIs."

  4. Why this company
     - "I have followed your company and I am impressed by your products.
        My skills align well with your team's work."

  5. Confident Closing
     - "I am genuinely excited about this opportunity and I am ready to contribute
        from day one."

  6. Note:
     - 60-90 seconds max
     - Practice OUT LOUD 10 times
     - Never say "Myself Aslam"
     - Smile & maintain eye contact

# How to Prepare Before the Interview: Preparation is the diff b/w nervous and confident
  1. Research the Company
     - Visit website - understand products & services
     - Check LinkedIn for recent news & updates
     - Prepare: "What do you know about us?"

  2. Read your own Resume
     - Every line is fair game for questions
     - Be ready to explain every project in detail
     - Read it carefully the night before

  3. Prepare Documents
     - 3 printed resume copies (in-person)
     - Marksheets, ID proof, certificates
     - PDF resume open in desktop (online)

  4. Dress Professionally
     - Formal always - ironed shirt & trousers
     - Online: formal top, clean background
     - Camera at eye level. Good lightning

  5. Practice Out Loud
     - Record yourself & listen back
     - Practice self into 10+ times
     - Check internet, camera & mic beforehand


# Body Language & Confidence: 60% of communication is non-verbal - make it count

  1. Eye Contact
     - Do:
       - Natural confident eye contact.
       - Online - look at Camera, not screen.
     - Don't:
       - Staring or constantly looking down or away.

  2. Posture
     - Do:
       - Sit straight, lean slightly forward.
       - Both feet flat on floor.
     - Don't:
       - Slouching, crossed arms, leaning too far back

  3. Hand Gestures
     - Do:
       - Hands on table or lap.
       - Natural gestures are fine and show confidence.
     - Don't:
       - Fidgeting, tapping fingers, playing with pen or hair

  4. Facial Expression
     - Do:
       - Warm, genuine smile when entering and at appropriate moments
     - Don't:
       - Blank or overly serious face throughout the conversation

  5. Voice & Pace
     - Do:
       - Speak clearly at moderate pace.
       - Pause 2-3 secs before answering.
     - Don't:
       - Speaking too fast.
       - Too many umms, ahhs, basically.

  6. Online Interviews:
     - Do:
       - Camera at eye level.
       - Clean Background
       - Good lighting
       - Mute when not speaking
     - Don't:
       - Looking down at screen.
       - Nosiy or cluttered background.

# Answering Common HR Question: Every question has a formula - learn the formula, never go blank again
  1. Tell me about yourself
     - Formula: Who > Skills > Projects > Why this Company > Confident close
     - "I am Rahul Sharma, B.Tech CS from ABC University. 
        I specialize in Python & full-stack dev.
        I built an e-commerce platform handling 500+ users.
        I admire your products and believe my skills again well.
        I am ready to contribute from day one."

  2. What are your strengths?
     - Formula: Name the strength > Give a real example > Connect to the role
     - "Problem solving is a key strength.
        When a critical bug appeared 3 days before our project deadline, I stayed back,
        debugged it overnight and we submitted on time. That composure under pressure
        is what I bring to this role."

  3. What are your weakness?
     - Formula: Real weakness > Self-awareness > What you are doing to improve
     - "I used to struggle with public speaking. I recognized it early, joined the 
        college debate club, and volunteered to present in class. I have improved
        significantly and I continue to work on it actively."

  4. Why do you want to join us?
     - Formula: Specific company knowledge > Link to your skills > Genuine Enthusiasm
     - "I have been following your company - I am impressed by your fintech products
        and your recent launch. The problem you are solving is fascinating, and my
        Python & API skills align well with your team..."

  5. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
     - Formula: Show ambition > Show commitment > Connect to this company's growth
     - "I see myself growing into a strong software engineer and deep domain expertise,
        taking on more responsibility, and moving into a leadership role. I believe
        this company is the right place to build that journey."

# Handling Tough & Tricky Questions: Test Composure - not just knowledge
  - Golden Rule: Pause > Breather > Think 2-3sec > Then answer.
  - Never badmouth your college, professors, or past internship - interviewer notice
    how you speak about others.

  1. Why should we hire you?
     - Strategy: 
       - Connect your specific skills to their needs.
       - Add unique value
       - Close with enthusiasm
     - "I bring Python & full-stack skills with real project experience - an e-commerce
        platform handling 500+ users. I take ownership, I am genuinely excited about
        the problems your team solves. I can contribute from day one."
  2. What is your salary expectation?
     - Strategy: 
       - Reached beforehand
       - Give a range, not a fixed number.
       - Show flexibility.
     - "Based on my research, I am expecting 3-5 LPA. However, I am open to discussion
        based on the overall package, growth opportunities, and responsibilites the
        role offers."
  3. Do you have other offers/interviews?
     - Strategy: 
       - Be honest, but strategic.
       - Show genuine interest in THIS company specifically.
     - "Yes, I am exploring a few opportunities. But I want to be honest - this company
        is my top preference because of the work you do and the growth environment here.
        I am genuienly excited about this role."
  4. Do you have any questions for us?
     - Strategy: 
       - ALWAYS say yes.
       - Prepare 2-3 smart questions.
       - Never ask about your salary in round 1
     - "Good questions: 
       - 'What does a typical day look like in this role?'
       - 'What are growth opportunities for someone starting here?'
       - 'What is the team culture like?'

# Interview Do's:
  a. Arrive 15min early (in-person) or log in 5 mins early (online)
  b. Greet everyone professionally - receptionist, HR, and panelist
  c. Listen fully before answering. Clarify if you don't understand the question
  d. Be honest - say 'I don't know but I am willing to learn'
  e. Send a thank you email/message to HR after the interview
  f. Ask about your next steps - 'When can I expect to hear back?'

# Note: If the interviewer asks about your notice period, say: 
  - "My notice period is 90 days, but it is negotiable."
  - "Once I receive the offer letter, I will negotiate with my manager or HR to try to
     reduce the notice period."
